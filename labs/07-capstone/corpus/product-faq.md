# Product FAQ

## Do you offer a warranty?

Every product ships with a 1-year manufacturer's warranty covering defects, not
damage from misuse. A warranty claim needs the order ID, so have it ready or use
the order lookup tool to find it.

## Can I change my order after placing it?

Orders can be changed or canceled only if they haven't shipped yet — check status
with the order lookup tool. Once an order shows as shipped, it can't be changed;
the return process is the path after delivery instead.

## Do you ship internationally?

Not yet — shipping is currently domestic only. International shipping is on the
roadmap but has no committed date.
