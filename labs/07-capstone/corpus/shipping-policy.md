# Shipping Policy

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days and is free on orders over $50. Expedited
shipping (1-2 business days) is available at checkout for an additional fee.

## Tracking

Every order gets a tracking number by email once it ships. Use the order lookup
tool with your order ID (e.g. A100) to check current status without digging up the
email.

## Delays

Backordered items ship as soon as they're back in stock; the order lookup tool
shows an estimated ETA for any backordered order. We don't charge for shipping on
delayed items, regardless of the original shipping tier selected.
